Interoperability

Keep the authority in the right tool.

FS prepares and documents the structural model. Final analysis and design acceptance remain with the selected solver and the responsible engineer.

ETABSNative `.edb` is created through the installed ETABS API, with a governed solver payload and audit artifacts.
STAADGenerate a `.std` model and open it through the Windows file association when STAAD.Pro is available.
RevitUse the Revit 2027 bridge for native model creation, or use IFC for concrete coordination geometry.
DXF / IFCSave portable coordination output and open it in AutoCAD, Revit, Navisworks, or another compatible desktop application.
